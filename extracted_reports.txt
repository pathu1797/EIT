2Unified Subterranean Root MRI & AI Agronomist Platform
APSRON-V
❖ Problems Addressed:
• Current AI agricultural apps only scan leaves after visible symptoms appear, meaning over 60% of crop damage is already done. 
• Devastating fungal attacks (such as Telya, Fusarium wilt, and root rot) originate underground weeks before leaf wilting, making them 
invisible to traditional cameras. 
• Farmers face middleman fraud due to a lack of instant crop quality validation, resulting in unfairly low mandi prices. 
❖ Proposed Solution:
• We propose an end-to-end agritech ecosystem that fuses below-ground Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT) with above-ground AI 
visual scanning and market intelligence. 
• Subterranean EIT Ring: A portable, 16-electrode ring injects 10 kHz AC current to map root conductivity profiles and detect decay 
non-destructively before foliage fails. 
• AI Agronomist App: A mobile application that processes EIT voltage matrices, scans leaves for active blight, and integrates DHT22 
micro-weather sensors to generate dynamic spray schedules.
• Market Engine: The app evaluates harvested fruits via a 5-second video scan to assign an AI Mandi Grade (A/B/C) and matches it with live 
e-NAM rates to guarantee fair pricing. 
❖ Innovation and Uniqueness: 
 Proactive Root Imaging: Non-destructive EIT decay detection. Handheld Multi-Sensor: Portable probe for leaf and fruit 
disease detection.
Micro-Weather Forecasts: 48-hour advance disease alerts. Live e-NAM Integration: Real-time market price matching
Automated Mandi Grading: AI crop grading to prevent fraud. Edge AI Processing: Offline scanning via TensorFlow Lite.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
4Unified Subterranean Root MRI & AI Agronomist Platform
APSRON-V
Project Feasibility
● Technically Feasible: Uses U-Net CNN to overcome non-linear math solvers, 
enabling accurate EIT voltage-to-image reconstruction despite soil noise.
● Economically Viable: Offers a budget-friendly alternative to lab-grade imaging; 
supports an affordable micro-fee rental model.
● Scalable & Operational: Features 18650 Li-Ion battery with 5V solar charging for 
off-grid, remote deployment.
● Practical: Enables non-destructive root decay detection and AI-driven grading to 
protect crop health and prevent market exploitation.
Challenges & Strategies
● Dry Soil Contact Resistance: Addressed by using saline gel on stainless steel 
electrodes and an Improved Howland Current Pump for stable 2mA AC injection.
● Sim2Real Gap (Inverse Problem Noise): Solved by pre-training on pyEIT synthetic 
meshes followed by transfer learning using 50–100 real-world baseline scans.
● Hardware Setup Time: Mitigated by employing a quick-clamping, flexible 
16-electrode collar for rapid, rigid deployment.
